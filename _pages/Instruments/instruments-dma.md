## Overview

The TA Instruments DMA 850 measures viscoelastic and mechanical properties under controlled temperature and environment. Two configurations are available: one with an advanced climate system (ACS) for dry-gas temperature sweeps, and one with humidity control for RH-dependent modulus measurements.

## Representative Specifications

- Model: TA Instruments DMA 850
- Temperature range: approximately -150 °C to 600 °C
- Force range: sub-millinewton resolution to several newtons
- Frequency range: 0.01 to 200 Hz
- Test modes: tension, bending, compression, shear
- Atmosphere control: dry-gas ACS and humidity/RH conditioning

## Tips
- Match fixtures and sample geometry carefully to the chosen deformation mode.
- Precondition humidity samples before dynamic testing to avoid transient drift.
- Start with conservative strain levels to stay within the linear viscoelastic regime.
