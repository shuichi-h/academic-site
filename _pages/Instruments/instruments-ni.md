## Overview

The Bruker TI Premier nanoindenter is used for instrumented indentation tests on polymers, coatings, and hard materials. It provides high-resolution load-displacement data for hardness and modulus measurements.

## Representative Specifications

- Model: Bruker TI Premier
- Load range: micro- to millinewton
- Displacement resolution: nanometer-scale
- Typical indenter types: Berkovich, spherical
- Test modes: load-control, displacement-control, continuous stiffness measurement

## Tips
- The nanoindenter tip is sensitive; avoid touching the probe without authorization.
- Verify sample surface cleanliness and flatness before each test.
- Use conservative loads for soft or thin specimens to avoid substrate effects.
