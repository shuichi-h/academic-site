## Overview

The Mettler Toledo DSC 5+ measures heat flow associated with thermal transitions such as glass transition, melting, crystallization, and cure reactions. It is used for polymers, pharmaceuticals, composites, and materials research that require accurate thermal analysis.

## Representative Specifications

- Model: Mettler Toledo DSC 5+
- Temperature range: approximately -90 °C to 600 °C
- Temperature accuracy: ±0.1 K
- Heating / cooling rate: 0.01 to 50 K/min
- Heat-flow sensitivity: around 0.1 µW
- Atmosphere control: nitrogen or dry air flow

## Tips
- Ensure sample pans are sealed and the reference pan is clean before each run.
- Use a low-mass calibration standard when measuring subtle transitions.
- Avoid placing samples too close to the sensor surfaces to keep thermal contact consistent.
