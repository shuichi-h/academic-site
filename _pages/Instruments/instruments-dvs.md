## Overview

Two DVS systems are available for moisture sorption analysis: the Surface Measurement Systems Advantage and the TA Discovery SA. Both instruments are used for sorption/desorption isotherms, humidity-controlled conditioning, and precise mass-change tracking of powders, films, and coated materials.

## Representative Specifications

- Models: SMS Advantage, TA Discovery SA
- Relative humidity range: 0.1–98% RH
- Temperature range: room temperature to about 60 °C
- Balance resolution: 0.1 µg
- Typical sample mass: up to 200 mg
- Atmosphere control: programmable dry/nitrogen purge with RH setpoints

## Tips
- Condition samples before measurement to establish a stable baseline.
- Use the smallest sample size that still yields a measurable mass change.
- Verify purge gas flow and humidity generator settings before starting an RH sequence.
